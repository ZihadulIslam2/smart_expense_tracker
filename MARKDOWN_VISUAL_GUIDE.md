## 📱 Dashboard - Savings Goals Card

### BEFORE:

```
Save an additional $200 per month to reach your 20% savings goal ($1,000/month total).
Cut Food expenses from $1,750 to $1,600 (save $150/month). Try meal prepping on Sundays
and limit dining out to once per week. You can reach 20% savings rate in 3 months with
these changes. Make savings automatic by setting up transfers on payday.
```

### AFTER:

```
# Monthly Savings Target

Save an additional **$200 per month** to reach your 20% savings goal
($1,000/month total).

# Reduce Food Spending

  • Cut Food expenses from $1,750 to $1,600 (save $150/month)
  • Try meal prepping on Sundays
  • Limit dining out to once per week

# Timeline to Goal

You can reach 20% savings rate in **3 months** with these changes.

# Bonus Habit

Make savings automatic by setting up transfers on payday.
```

## ✨ Formatting Examples

### Headers Transform:

```
BEFORE: # Savings Goals & Tips
AFTER:  [Large blue text, bold] Savings Goals & Tips

BEFORE: ## Monthly Target
AFTER:  [Medium blue text, bold] Monthly Target

BEFORE: ### Details
AFTER:  [Smaller blue text, bold] Details
```

### Bold Text:

```
BEFORE: Save $200 per month
AFTER:  Save **$200 per month**  [becomes bold + colored]
```

### Bullet Points:

```
BEFORE: - Cut Food spending by $150/month
AFTER:
        • Cut Food spending by $150/month
        (with blue bullet and proper indentation)
```

### Numbered Lists:

```
BEFORE: 1. Monthly Savings Target
AFTER:
        1.  [blue bold] Monthly Savings Target
        2.  [blue bold] Reduce Food Spending
        3.  [blue bold] Timeline to Goal
```
