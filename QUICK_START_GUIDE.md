## 3️⃣ Smart Notifications

### What You'll See:

- 🔔 Daily expense tracking reminders
- 🎯 Goal progress updates
- ⚠️ Budget alerts
- 🎉 Savings milestone celebrations

### How to Enable:

1. Open app → Click **Settings** icon (top right)
2. Toggle "Enable Notifications" ON
3. Enable "Daily Expense Reminder"
4. Set your preferred time (default: 8:00 PM)
5. Wait for your first reminder!

### Notification Types:

```
Daily: "Don't forget to track your expenses! 💰"
Goal:  "You're 80% close to your savings goal! 🎯"
Alert: "⚠️ You've used 90% of your Food budget"
Milestone: "🎉 You've saved $1,000!"
```

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Notifications Not Working?

**Android:**

1. Settings → Enable Notifications
2. Phone Settings → Apps → Smart Expense Tracker → Notifications → Allow
3. Make sure "Do Not Disturb" is off

**iOS:**

1. Settings → Enable Notifications
2. iPhone Settings → Smart Expense Tracker → Notifications → Allow
3. Check notification permissions

### Can't See New Screens?

**Solution:** Make sure you've updated the app

1. Close app completely
2. Reopen app
3. Look for Education and Settings icons

### Theme Not Applied?

**Solution:** Restart the app

1. Close app
2. Run: `flutter run --hot-reload`
3. Or fully restart app
